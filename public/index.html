How the Top-Leader Threshold & Proportional Split Works

Unlike fixed-goal challenges where everyone can coast, StakeFit anchors the survival threshold to your group's #1 performer (\(S_{\max}\)).

Step 1: Dynamic Survival Cutoff (\(C\))
Cutoff = Top_Score × 70%

Any friend whose score \(S_i < C\) fails to keep pace with the leader.

Step 2: Forfeited Penalty Pool (\(P_{\text{forfeit}}\))
Pool = (Failed Friends) × BuyIn × Slash%

Friends below the cutoff lose the configured percentage of their entry fee into the reward pot.

Step 3: Achievement-Weighted Redistribution
Bonus_i = Pool × (Score_i / Sum_Qualifiers_Scores)

Every surviving friend keeps their buy-in AND earns a share of the forfeited pool strictly proportional to how much they achieved!

Edge-Case Safeguard: If everyone stays above the cutoff percentage of #1 (zero dropouts), everyone safely reclaims 100% of their buy-in—or switch to "All-In Pot Mode" so the entire pot is always distributed by achievement ratio!

🏦 Enterprise Zero-Trust Fitness & Escrow Architecture

How We Guarantee Trust, Profile Isolation & Automatic Phone Sync

When real money is in the pot, self-reported text inputs cannot be trusted. StakeFit Enterprise enforces 1) OAuth2 / Passkey Row-Level Security (RBAC) so members can only access their own profile, 2) Hardware-Attested Background Phone Sync with zero manual typing, and 3) Multi-Sensor Biometric Anomaly Detection to block spoofing.

1. Strict RBAC Isolation
JWT + Row-Level Security

Each member authenticates via Passkey/OAuth2. API mutations verify jwt.sub === member.id. Members cannot edit or log data for any other member, and challenge rules are smart-contract locked after start.

2. Native Phone Bridges
HealthKit & Health Connect

iOS & Android background daemons query HKHealthStore / Health Connect and enforce wasUserEntered == false, automatically discarding any manually typed steps or calories in the phone's health app.

3. Cloud Wearable APIs
Terra / Vital / Rook Webhooks

Direct server-to-server OAuth webhooks from Garmin, Strava, WHOOP, Fitbit & Oura signed with HMAC-SHA256. Zero user intervention—workouts appear seconds after completion.

4. Biometric Anti-Cheat
Cadence × HR × GPS AI

Detects mechanical "phone shakers" and GPS spoofing by requiring physiological correlation between step cadence, optical heart rate (BPM), accelerometer variance, and overlapping device deduplication.
